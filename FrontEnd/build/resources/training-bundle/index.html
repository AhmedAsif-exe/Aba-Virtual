About the Trainer

Niha Khan is a Qualified Behavior Analyst (QBA) with a Master’s degree in Developmental and Educational Psychology from University College London (UCL). She specializes in working with children aged 2 to 16 years, supporting their developmental, behavioral, and emotional needs through evidence-based, compassionate care.

While inspiring many, she has contributed in providing individualized behaviour support for children and adolescents, promoting parent coaching and training to build effective strategies at home and supervised QBA/ABAT candidates, ensuring ethical and high-quality practice. Not limiting her potentials, she has delivered informative seminars and workshops focused on key topics in child development, behavioral support, and positive parenting.
