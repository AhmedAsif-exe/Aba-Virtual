Meet the founder

Faiza Faizan

ABA Virtual was founded by Faiza Faizan, a Qualified Autism Services Practitioner–Supervisor (QASP-S) from Pakistan. ABA Virtual is built on experience and learning: here, we give caregivers and professionals who support neurodivergent learners personalised guidance that brings clarity and structure to everyday challenges. As a former resource teacher, Faiza has always enjoyed creating resources, and we hope you enjoy them too!
